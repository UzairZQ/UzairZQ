## About Me

Software engineer based in Frankfurt, Germany. I build offline-first mobile applications with a focus on clean architecture and on-device AI. Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of Flutter and machine learning, figuring out how to bring AI capabilities to users without depending on a server.

- 🎓 Pursuing M.Sc. in High Integrity Systems at Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences
- 🤖 Currently building **ContextShift**, an offline-first productivity app with a JARVIS assistant powered by on-device Google Gemma
- 💼 Open to Werkstudent and internship roles in Flutter, Python, or AI engineering
- 📍 Frankfurt am Main, Germany
- 🌐 Languages: English (C1), German (B1), Urdu (native)
