## About Me

I am a software engineer based in Frankfurt, Germany, focused on building reliable, user-friendly mobile applications. My work is mainly centered around Flutter, clean architecture, offline-first systems, and practical AI features that run close to the user.

I enjoy turning complex ideas into polished products, especially apps that feel fast, useful, and maintainable.

- Pursuing M.Sc. in High Integrity Systems at Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences  
- Building mobile apps with Flutter, Dart, Firebase, SQLite, and clean architecture  
- Exploring on-device AI, local-first workflows, and intelligent mobile experiences  
- Open to Werkstudent and internship roles in Flutter, Python, mobile, or AI engineering  
- Based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany  
- Languages: English C1, German B1, Urdu native
